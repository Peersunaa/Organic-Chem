# Lesson 17 "Predict the product" plan (2026-10-20)

Goal: a review lesson (id 17, slug predict-product, chapter 'review', sections []) that teaches the three questions, derives every one of the 38 hub cards before the card is shown, names the exceptions, and rehearses exam wording.

Files touched (only these): learn/lessons/17-predict-product.js (new), learn/lessons/index.js, learn/engine/recap.js, learn/app.js (one home link), learn/tests/lesson17.mjs (new, not wired into run.mjs).

## Shape
- Part 1, three questions (9 steps): Q1 rich -> poor, Q2 where H goes, Q3 same side / opposite sides. Each: worked (learner picks the moves), explain (<= 80 words), predict.
- Part 2, derive every family (38 card steps + labs + 3 kind cards). One `predict` per hub card on a fresh substrate, drawn options, then a reveal that says which question gave the card's three facts (adds = Q1, where = Q2, space = Q3), names the product KIND and links `#reactions/<id>`. Groups: HX and water; anti additions; syn / oxidations; alkynes; dienes; benzene EAS; directors; side chains; alcohol oxidations. A reaction-lab step closes the alkene, alkyne, diene and substituted-ring groups.
- Part 3, exceptions (7 `choice` steps): hydroboration, halogens, PCC, amine in acid, Clemmensen, Birch, Diels-Alder regiochemistry.
- Part 4, exam wording (3 `predict` steps with drawn options): alkene with stereochemistry, aromatic with two directors, alkyne.
- Mastery `{ count: 5, generator: 'predict-product' }` (run.mjs still demands count 3: orchestrator to relax). useIt: book [] + note.

## Verifying SMILES
Every correct option carries `chk: { card, sub, v, halide? }`. The generator `engine/generators-predict.js` `solveCard` is the engine the drill uses; for the 10 fixed cards its right answer is the hub card's own example. tests/lesson17.mjs compares canonical SMILES (either mirror image when the product is a racemate). Wrong options are the drill's own distractors (same `why`), re-checked for parse, uniqueness and difference from the right answer. Part 3 and 4 steps use the same checks or an independent RDKit transform written in the test.

## Tests (tests/lesson17.mjs)
Schema per step, SMILES parse, engine equality, every `#reactions/<id>` and `#lesson-NN/sK` link resolves, all 38 card ids referenced in Part 2, recap entries resolve, mastery generator exists.
