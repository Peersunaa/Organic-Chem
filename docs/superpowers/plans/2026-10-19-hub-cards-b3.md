# Hub cards to add from workbook B3.1 (orchestrator-verified chemistry, 2026-10-19)

Peera confirmed these come out on the midterm even though the lessons do not teach them. Each card follows the hub's four-part structure. The card IS the teaching: the "what" line may be two sentences, and a card without a lesson link shows "Not in a lesson: this card is the short version" instead of the link. Example SMILES below were parsed in RDKit; the builder keeps them unless RDKit disagrees, and writes RDKit parse + formula tests for each (no engine model exists for any of these; fixed answers only).

| id | group | reagent | example substrate → product (SMILES) | facts: adds / where / space |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| diels-alder | diene | Conjugated diene + a C=C with an electron-withdrawing group (dienophile), heat | `C=C/C=C/C` + `C=CC=O` → `O=CC1C(C)C=CCC1` (penta-1,3-diene + acrolein → 2-methylcyclohex-3-ene-1-carbaldehyde) | adds: a new six-membered ring (two new C–C σ bonds, one new C=C between old C2–C3) / where: diene C1 and C4 bond to the two dienophile carbons; a 1-substituted diene gives the "1,2" (ortho-like) product, a 2-substituted diene gives the "1,4" (para-like) product / space: syn: groups that are cis on the dienophile stay cis in the ring |
| clemmensen | arene | Zn(Hg), HCl, heat (Clemmensen) | `CC(=O)c1ccccc1` → `CCc1ccccc1` (acetophenone → ethylbenzene) | adds: replaces the C=O of an aryl ketone with CH₂ / where: only the carbonyl next to the ring / space: n.a. Steps mention: pairs with Friedel–Crafts acylation to make straight-chain alkylbenzenes without rearrangement; Wolff–Kishner (H₂NNH₂, KOH) does the same job. |
| birch | arene | Na, liquid NH₃, EtOH (Birch) | `OC(=O)c1ccccc1` → `OC(=O)C1C=CCC=C1` (benzoic acid → cyclohexa-2,5-diene-1-carboxylic acid); second example `COc1ccccc1` → `COC1=CCC=CC1` | adds: two H atoms, para to each other, giving a 1,4-cyclohexadiene / where: an electron-withdrawing group (CO₂H, CO₂R) sits on an sp³ (reduced) carbon; an electron-donating group (OCH₃, alkyl) sits on an sp² (C=C) carbon / space: n.a. |
| alkyne-hydroboration | alkyne | 1) BH₃ (or B₂H₆, or disiamylborane) 2) H₂O₂, NaOH | `CCCCC#C` → `CCCCCC=O` (hex-1-yne → hexanal) | adds: H and OH across the triple bond, enol → aldehyde (tautomerises) / where: anti-Markovnikov: OH on the END carbon, so a terminal alkyne gives an ALDEHYDE (contrast: Hg²⁺/H₂O gives the methyl ketone); internal alkyne gives a ketone / space: syn (not usually marked) |
| alkene-hydroboration | alkene | 1) BH₃ · THF 2) H₂O₂, NaOH | `CC1=CCCCC1` → `C[C@@H]1CCCC[C@H]1O` (1-methylcyclohexene → trans-2-methylcyclohexanol, racemic) | adds: H and OH / where: anti-Markovnikov: OH on the carbon with MORE H / space: syn: H and OH add to the same face, so OH ends up trans to the CH₃ |
| benzylic-bromination | arene | Br₂, hν (light) or NBS, heat | `CCCc1ccccc1` → `CCC(Br)c1ccccc1` (propylbenzene → (1-bromopropyl)benzene, racemic) | adds: Br replaces one H / where: ONLY the benzylic carbon (the one attached to the ring), because the benzylic radical is resonance-stabilised; no Br on the ring without FeBr₃ / space: racemate if the benzylic carbon becomes a stereocentre |
| oxidation-pcc | arene (listed under "Alcohols, side chains") | PCC, CH₂Cl₂, room temp | `OC(CCCO)c1ccccc1` → `O=CCCC(=O)c1ccccc1` (1-phenylbutane-1,4-diol → 4-oxo-4-phenylbutanal) | adds: removes two H (C–OH → C=O) / where: 1° alcohol → aldehyde (stops there), 2° alcohol → ketone, 3° alcohol → no reaction / space: n.a. |
| oxidation-cr | arene (same group) | Na₂Cr₂O₇, H₂SO₄, H₂O (or CrO₃/H₃O⁺, or KMnO₄) | `CC(O)c1ccccc1` → `CC(=O)c1ccccc1` (1-phenylethanol → acetophenone); second example `CCCCO` → `CCCC(=O)O` | adds: removes two H, and a 1° alcohol goes all the way / where: 1° alcohol → CARBOXYLIC ACID (not aldehyde), 2° alcohol → ketone, 3° → no reaction / space: n.a. |
| amine-in-acid | arene (directors) | HNO₃, H₂SO₄ on an aniline (–NH₂, –NR₂) | `CN(C)c1ccc(C)cc1` → `CN(C)c1ccc(C)c([N+](=O)[O-])c1` (N,N-dimethyl-4-methylaniline → nitro META to the nitrogen, ortho to CH₃) | adds: NO₂ replaces a ring H / where: in strong acid the amine is protonated to –NR₂H⁺, which has no lone pair to give, so it becomes a META director and deactivator; the CH₃ still directs ortho/para, and here both agree / space: n.a. Steps: 1) ask "is the medium strongly acidic?" 2) if yes, treat –NR₂ as –NR₂H⁺ = meta director 3) combine with the other director 4) note that some para product forms from the small amount of free amine. |
| desulfonation | arene | Dilute H₂SO₄ (or D₂SO₄/D₂O), heat: reverse of sulfonation | `Cc1ccc(S(=O)(=O)O)cc1` → `Cc1ccc([2H])cc1` (4-methylbenzenesulfonic acid → toluene-4-d) | adds: H (or D) replaces –SO₃H / where: exactly where the SO₃H was / space: n.a. Steps: sulfonation is reversible; SO₃H is used as a temporary blocking group (put it para, do the reaction ortho, remove it) — links to the aromatic synthesis steps in Lesson 14. |

Group placement: put oxidation-pcc and oxidation-cr under the aromatics group with a sub-header "Side chains and alcohols (not in the book)". diels-alder joins dienes. Hydroboration cards join alkenes and alkynes.

Lesson links: amine-in-acid → Lesson 14 director steps; desulfonation → Lesson 14 synthesis/blocking step if one exists, else Lesson 13 sulfonation step; benzylic-bromination → Lesson 13 side-chain step; the rest have no lesson and show the "Not in a lesson" line.
